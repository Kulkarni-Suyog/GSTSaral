## 2. Summary Findings & Production Default Decision

- **Accuracy & Hallucination Control:** Both v1 and v2 scored **10/10** on factual grounding and properly returned `"confidence": "low"` on **Q6** (GSTR-9C penalty) without inventing figures.
- **Clarity & Trust:** **Prompt v2 (`task_v2_structured.txt`)** outperformed v1 on **9 of 10 questions** because separating **Verdict / Before vs. After / Source** prevents shopkeepers from confusing old and new rates and builds trust via explicit PIB citation.
- **Default Choice:** `/chat` defaults to `prompt_version="v2"`, while keeping `prompt_version="v1"` available via request payload for compact UI cards or low-bandwidth/SMS integrations.
