# GSTSaral

**GSTSaral** is an AI-powered Goods and Services Tax (GST) assistant built to simplify Indian tax compliance, rate lookups, and filing rules in plain language.

## Project Card

| Field | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Product** | **GSTSaral** — Conversational GST Compliance & Rate Assistant |
| **Target User** | Small business owners, MSMEs, freelancers, and local traders in India |
| **Problem** | Navigating multi-tier GST slabs (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%), registration thresholds, and HSN/SAC rules is complex and overwhelming without hiring a full-time Chartered Accountant. |
| **Data Source** | Official Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) notifications, GST Council rate schedules, and CGST/IGST Act FAQs |
